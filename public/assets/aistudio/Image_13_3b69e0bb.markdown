## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, elite corporate identity for a premier Indian consulting firm operating across two symbiotic pillars: Financial Services and AI & Technology Solutions. The visual aesthetic bridges institutional stability with cutting-edge engineering precision.

### Personality & Values
- **Authoritative Clarity:** Communicating institutional rigor, regulatory compliance, and fiduciary duty without corporate bloat.
- **Architectural Precision:** Clean lines, deliberate structure, and balanced whitespace reflecting algorithmic and quantitative discipline.
- **Progressive Sophistication:** Unifying legacy wealth management sensibilities with modern AI-driven enterprise transformation.

### Visual Style
A tailored blend of **Corporate Modernism** and **Refined Architectural Glass**. The aesthetic emphasizes structural honesty, razor-thin borders (1px hairline rules), subtle backdrop blurs (frosting) over content-aware containers, and high typographic contrast. Avoid unnecessary skeuomorphism, cartoonish illustrations, or noisy drop-shadows. The interface must read as an executive-tier, audit-ready tool that breeds institutional investor and enterprise client confidence.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined monochromatic spectrum of forest, pine, and emerald greens, anchored by warm enterprise slates and immaculate alabaster neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0D382A` - Deep Forest Green):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary branding, executive headers, primary button backgrounds, and strong typographic anchors. Evokes sovereign wealth, growth, and enduring trust.
- **Secondary (`#00A86B` - Precision Emerald):** The technological pulse. Denotes action, affirmative metrics, AI-driven computation, focus rings, and high-value conversion triggers. Maintained at WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast ratios against dark surfaces and darkened to `#09734B` when set over light neutral backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#1E293B` - Deep Slate):** Grounding neutral dark. Serves as high-contrast body typography, code accents, and technical data visualizations for AI services.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAF8` - Warm Alabaster):** Pure, warm canvas base offering high eye-comfort during long analytical reading sessions. Secondary surface fills use `#EEF3EF` and `#E2EBE5`.

### Dual-Division Modulation
- **Financial Services Division:** Employs deeper primary forest tones (`#0D382A`, `#08231A`), muted sage hair lines (`#CFDDD3`), and dark slate typography. Signals compliance, conservatism, and risk mitigation.
- **AI & Technology Solutions Division:** Activates precision emerald glows, deep slate surfaces with 8% emerald tinting, dark surface containers (`#0A1410`), and technical mono accents to signal engineering power and intelligent automation.

## Typography

Typography establishes executive poise and data clarity across marketing funnels and deep advisory briefs.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Geometric yet sculpted, Outfit provides an authoritative, forward-leaning executive presence without feeling aggressive. High tracking reduction at larger display sizes keeps phrases tight and commanding.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for warm, open counters, ergonomic legibility, and high render quality on retina screens across extensive case studies and capability whitepapers.
- **Labels & Functional UI (Inter):** Systematically neutral, utilitarian, and optimized for tabular figures, operational tags, division indicators, and numeric KPI cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a structural 12-column grid system built on an 8px base rhythm. It balances generous executive whitespace with high-density informational tables.

### Structural Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, fixed max content container width of 1240px, `margin` of 4rem, `gutter` of 1.5rem.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, fluid container width, `margin-tablet` of 2rem, `gutter` of 1.5rem.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, fluid container width, `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem, `gutter-mobile` of 1rem.

### Sectional Rhythms
- Marketing landing sections use vertical padding of `space-3xl` (6rem) for high impact and breathability.
- Feature grids, lead generation workflows, and consulting case modules employ internal stacking gaps of `space-xl` (2.5rem) to preserve clear hierarchical separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural translucency, hairline structural framing, and soft ambient shadows rather than dramatic drop-shadows.

### Layering Principles
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid warm alabaster (`#F8FAF8`) for light mode or deep green-slate (`#061610`) for specialized technical dark mode segments.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Light semi-opaque fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with a backdrop filter blur of `12px` and a 1px solid border (`#E2EBE5`).
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Flyouts & Navigation):** Fixed glass headers utilize `rgba(248, 250, 248, 0.88)` with `16px` backdrop blur, anchored by a bottom hairline (`rgba(13, 56, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Modals & Interactive Overlays):** Ambient shadow configured as `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(13, 56, 42, 0.12)` layered with a 1px border (`rgba(0, 168, 107, 0.2)`).

Shadows are universally tinted with deep forest green pigments rather than generic blacks, grounding elevation within the brand palette.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts Level 1 (Soft). All UI elements feature tight, calculated corner radii that communicate discipline, architectural precision, and institutional seriousness.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Used for micro-badges, form controls, check elements, and status chips.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px - `rounded-lg`):** Standard for core content cards, dialogs, form containers, and interactive service panels.
- **Executive Radius (0.75rem / 12px - `rounded-xl`):** Dedicated to high-level division showcase heroes and featured transformation case studies.
- **Capsule / Pill Radius (9999px):** Strictly reserved for division taxonomy badges, status indicators, and floating utility pills. Buttons remain structural rectangles with 4px or 8px corners to preserve corporate stature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Lead Capture / Schedule Consultation):** Deep Forest Green background (`#0D382A`), high-contrast white text, 8px radius, `padding: 0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#134E3B` with an inner hairline highlight.
- **Secondary Action (Explore Division):** Transparent surface with a 1px solid border (`#0D382A` or `#CFDDD3`), `#0D382A` text, expanding emerald arrow indicator on hover.
- **Tertiary / Utility Action:** Transparent background, slate text (`#1E293B`), active emerald text on hover with an animated 1.5px baseline rule.

### Badges & Division Chips
- **Financial Services Division Badge:** Slate-forest pill surface (`rgba(13, 56, 42, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(13, 56, 42, 0.15)`), text styled with `label-xs` uppercase in Deep Forest Green, anchored by a solid geometric square icon.
- **AI & Technology Solutions Division Badge:** Light emerald glass pill (`rgba(0, 168, 107, 0.08)`), 1px border (`rgba(0, 168, 107, 0.3)`), text in Precision Emerald (`#09734B`), anchored by a 6px glowing status dot.

### Form Inputs & Lead Intake
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** 8px radius, background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `#D4DFD8`. Active focus rings display a sharp 2px glow in `#00A86B` with zero offset. Labels sit consistently above inputs in `label-md` using tertiary slate (`#1E293B`).
- **Select Trigger Panels:** Integrated multi-step capability toggles enabling prospects to dynamically designate their inquiry between "Financial Regulatory & Advisory" and "AI Enterprise Integration".

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Dual Division Cards:** 8px radius, 1px border in `#E2EBE5`, surface fill of `#FFFFFF`. Upon hover, the top border activates a 2px accent rule (Deep Forest for Finance, Precision Emerald for AI), accompanied by a subtle 4px vertical rise.
- **Metrics & KPI Displays:** Clean horizontal structures featuring numeric figures rendered in Outfit 36px bold, paired with micro-labels in Inter `label-xs` and a 1px vertical dividing border between metrics.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- 4px soft rounded corners for checkboxes, circular for radios. Inactive border 1.5px (`#94A3B8`). Selected state displays solid `#0D382A` with crisp white SVG checkmarks or secondary emerald fills for tech-specific multi-select modules.
